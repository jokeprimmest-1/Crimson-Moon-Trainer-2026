## 🎮 About the Game

Crimson Moon is a gothic action-adventure RPG developed by ProbablyMonsters and released on September 1, 2026. Players take the role of a Nephilim, a human-angel hybrid fighting demons, undead, vampires, and Hellgrowth abominations in the cursed city of Gildenarch.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Crimson Moon**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for Crimson Moon's mission-based action RPG combat. It can protect the Nephilim during difficult encounters, enhance offensive power, support build testing, and make solo or cooperative missions easier to manage.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Gildenarch with less difficulty, practice parries and finishers, test weapons and armor builds, or reduce the challenge of missions and boss encounters.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Load into a mission normally and use the trainer only after the player character is fully active. Defensive options are useful while moving through districts and wards, but they should be disabled before transitions or menus if the game behaves unexpectedly.

### Tools & Inventory

Use the Sanctus Clypeus hub and its Blacksmith to prepare armor and weapons before launching an incursion. The trainer is intended to support build testing rather than replace normal equipment management. Save changes normally and avoid switching equipment while a temporary trainer option is being activated.

### World & Environment

Missions are selected from the War Table in the Sanctus Clypeus. Progress through Gildenarch by clearing districts, pushing back Hellgrowth, completing quests, and confronting escalating threats from the Infernal Legion. Trainer options are best used after a mission has started and should be turned off before returning to the hub.

### Combat & Weapons

Crimson Moon emphasizes fast melee combat, parries, finishers, supernatural powers, angelic arts, and transformations. Use invincibility to study enemy patterns and parry timing, then enable damage assistance when you want to shorten encounters. Exercise extra caution during boss fights and online co-op sessions.
